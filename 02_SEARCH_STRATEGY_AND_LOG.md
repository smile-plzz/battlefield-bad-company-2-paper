## 1. Search Strategy Architecture

To establish an authoritative evidence base for an interdisciplinary game studies investigation, searches were executed across both scholarly academic repositories and primary industry/developer documentation archives:

### Scholarly Repositories & Citation Indexes
- Crossref API & OpenAlex Scholarly Database
- Google Scholar / Semantic Scholar
- Digital Games Research Association (DiGRA) Digital Library
- Oxford Academic (*Global Studies Quarterly*)
- SAGE Publications (*The British Journal of Politics and International Relations*, *Games and Culture*)
- Taylor & Francis (*Journal of Marketing Management*, *Critical Military Studies*, *The RUSI Journal*)
- Wiley Online Library (*Political Studies*, *Communication Theory*)
- MIT Press, Harvard University Press, NYU Press, Routledge academic monographs

### Primary Industry & Technical Design Archives
- GDC Vault (Game Developers Conference technical presentations)
- *Designing Sound* (Audio Director Stefan Strandberg exclusive engineering postmortems)
- *WorthPlaying* & *GameSpot* (Senior Producer Patrick Bach & Gordon Van Dyke development interviews)
- *NME* (Lead Designer & Campaign Writer David Goldfarb retrospective interviews)
- *Kotaku* & contemporary game design journalism (Brian Crecente Frostbite demonstrations)

## 3. Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

### Inclusion Criteria (IC)
- **IC1:** Peer-reviewed journal articles, scholarly book chapters, or academic monographs in game studies, media studies, political science, military sociology, or cultural theory addressing military videogames, militainment, procedural rhetoric, or the representation of modern conflict.
- **IC2:** Primary source interviews, design postmortems, GDC papers, or engineering retrospectives by verified members of the EA DICE development team (*Battlefield: Bad Company 2*).
- **IC3:** Rigorous studies examining environmental destruction, spatial design, sound design (HDR audio), squad mechanics, or competitive multiplayer systems relevant to FPS game design.
- **IC4:** Explicit comparative literature examining the genre rivalry and stylistic divergence between the *Battlefield* and *Call of Duty* franchises.

### Exclusion Criteria (EC)
- **EC1:** Non-scholarly player forum posts, consumer wikis, walkthrough guides, and speculative fan rumors.
- **EC2:** Promotional marketing press releases without substantive design or technical commentary.
- **EC3:** Sources confusing *Battlefield: Bad Company 2* with *Battlefield 3* (Frostbite 2.0) or conflating the core game with *Battlefield: Bad Company 2: Vietnam* without explicit distinction.
- **EC4:** Studies with unverified citations, broken DOIs, or fabricated empirical surveys.
